First stable release of XEU Forums, an unofficial Android app for the Xiaomi.EU community forum.

- Browse every forum and thread in a native Material 3 design, in light and dark mode
- Search threads and posts, and jump to new posts
- My devices: follow the phones you own and get one feed of their forums
- Log in, reply, quote and start threads; switch between several accounts
- Alerts, direct messages and watched threads inside the app, with a bell for new alerts
- Notifications for forum alerts and new direct messages
- In-app updates from GitHub (Stable and Dev channels) with a backup download path

Not affiliated with Xiaomi or Xiaomi.EU.
